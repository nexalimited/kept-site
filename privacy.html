Privacy

Kept for iOS · last updated 2 October 2026

Kept does not collect anything about you. Not your name, not your email, not what you save, not how you use the app. There is no account to create, and Nexa Limited runs no server that receives your data.

Where your things are kept

Everything you save — the text, the pictures, the videos, your notes, your tags, your collections — is stored on your device. By default it goes nowhere else. The developer cannot see it, cannot recover it, and has no way to reach it.

If you delete the app and have not turned on iCloud (below) or exported a backup, that data goes with it.

iCloud sync and backup (Kept Pro, optional)

Kept Pro can sync your saves between your iPhone and iPad and keep a backup. This is off until you turn it on: Kept asks once after you buy Pro, and you can switch it off any time in Settings. Free use never writes to iCloud.

When it is on, your items, notes, tags, collections, pictures and videos are stored in your own iCloud account, in Kept's private area of Apple's CloudKit. It is tied to your Apple Account and counts against your own iCloud storage. Nexa Limited has no access to it and cannot read it. The sample content shown on first launch is never uploaded.

Apple operates iCloud and handles that data under Apple's privacy policy.

Kept on the web (Kept Pro, optional)

keptsave.com shows a read-only copy of your timeline in a browser. It has no accounts or logins. It works only when you choose it: the page shows a QR code, you scan it in Kept and confirm, and Kept then sends an encrypted copy of your timeline through Apple's CloudKit to that browser.

The decryption key exists only in the QR code and in that browser's memory. It is never sent to Apple or to us, so neither can read the copy. The copy is deleted from CloudKit after 10 minutes, or as soon as you sign the browser out from your phone. keptsave.com is a static page; like any host, its provider may keep ordinary server logs of page requests.

The one time Kept uses the network

When you save a link, Kept tries to fetch the original text and picture so you keep a readable snapshot. To do that, your device asks that website directly — the same request your browser would make if you opened the link yourself.

That request goes to the site you saved, not to us. Nothing passes through a server we run, because we do not run one. Those sites may keep their own server logs, exactly as they would if you visited them normally.

If the fetch fails, Kept keeps the link and the text you shared and moves on.

What Kept does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No Kept server, and no copy of your data held by us — iCloud, if you turn it on, is your own
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because there is nothing held to sell or share

Topics

Kept files what you save under topics. That is worked out on your device by looking at the words in the text. Nothing is sent anywhere to do it, and you can change any topic by hand.

Photos and videos

A photo or video you share into Kept is copied into Kept's own storage on the device. Kept does not read your photo library and does not ask for access to it.

Children

Kept is rated 4+ and has no social features, no user-to-user content and no advertising. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Purchases and updates

Kept Pro is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; Kept never sees your card or payment details. Settings can check whether a newer version exists by asking the App Store, sending only Kept's public app number.

Home Screen widget

The widget reads a small list of your saves that Kept keeps on your device. It does not use the network.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kept ever sends anything new off the device, this page will say so before that version ships, the App Store privacy label will be updated to match, and the feature will be something you switch on yourself.
